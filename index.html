FAQ

Questions, answered.

What does the admin panel actually control?+

Everything that happens on your marketplace — vendor approvals and penalties, commission structures (global, vendor-specific, category-specific, or product-specific), abuse reports, store reviews, support conversations, verification workflows, B2B customer management and product tags.

Can I set different commissions for different vendors or categories?+

Yes. Set a global commission as your default, then override it at the vendor, category or even individual product level. Rules cascade automatically, and every change is logged.

How does vendor verification work?+

Every vendor goes through four checks — passport, address, phone number, and company profile. Each step is reviewed automatically where possible, with manual fallback for edge cases. Verified vendors earn a trust badge that's visible to shoppers.

Can I join a customer support conversation as the admin?+

Yes. Toggle your identity between Admin and Vendor mid-chat so shoppers always see the right voice. Escalations move from vendor queues to your inbox without losing any context.

Does the platform support B2B as well as retail?+

Yes. Run wholesale and retail on the same platform with dedicated B2B logins, gated pricing, RFQ workflows, min/max quantity rules and tiered wholesale discounts — all configured from the admin panel.

Who can create and approve product tags?+

Vendors can create their own tags for products and stores. You, as admin, can approve, merge or block any tag — and use them to power featured collections, trending searches and homepage placement.

Can I run bulk actions on multiple vendors at once?+

Yes. Filter vendors by any combination of status, category or performance metrics, then approve, suspend, penalize or email the whole selection in a single action.
